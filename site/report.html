Napple Online Report and Takedown Policy

Version: v1 · Effective: 2026-11-03 · Not legal advice.

Reporting a player

In the game's Online tab pick a player (friends list, recent players or a lobby) and choose Report, with a reason and optional note. The report links the profile and, if there was one, the match, so we can look at the 30-day match log (see the Privacy Policy). You can also block a player at any time; blocked players cannot see or contact you.

What we do

  • We review reports in good faith, usually within a few days; this is a volunteer project, so there is no guaranteed response time.
  • Outcomes: no action, warning, content removal, temporary or permanent suspension. Serious cases (threats, child safety, doxxing) are handled first and may be passed to the authorities.
  • Reporters are never named to the reported player. We do not tell you the detail of action taken against someone else.
  • If your account is suspended you can write to legal@napple.lat to appeal; a different reviewer looks at it when possible.
  • Reports made in bad faith, or repeated abuse of the report button, are themselves a violation of the Terms.

Takedown requests (content or copyright)

Napple Online hosts only small profile content (nickname, bio, links, a 256x256 photo) and encrypted saves it cannot read. If you believe content on a profile infringes your copyright or other rights, or breaks the law, email legal@napple.lat with:

  1. who you are and how to reach you;
  2. where the content is (nickname or friend code);
  3. what right it infringes and why;
  4. a statement that you act in good faith and the information is accurate (for copyright, under penalty of perjury if you act under the US DMCA).

We remove clearly infringing or illegal content promptly, tell the account owner, and allow a counter-notice. Repeat infringers lose their accounts. Requests about Nintendo material in the Napple app or repository are handled through the repository's issue tracker or legal@napple.lat.

legal@napple.lat is a placeholder to be confirmed by the project owner before launch.
