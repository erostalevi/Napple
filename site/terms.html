You may only use Napple with games, keys and firmware you legally own and dumped yourself. Napple does not provide, host or link to any of them. Do not use the service to share or request game files, keys, firmware or built apps.

3. Age

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. If your local law sets a higher age for consent to online services (for example 16 in some EU countries), you must be at least that age or have a parent or guardian's permission. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under the minimum age, we will delete it.

4. Your account

  • Give a real email address you control, and keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • One person, up to 8 profiles per account. Nicknames must be unique and must not impersonate other people, the Napple team or Nintendo.
  • Tell us at legal@napple.lat if you think your account was taken over.

5. Acceptable use

Do not:

  • harass, threaten, hate on or dox other players, or post sexual content involving minors (reported to the authorities);

  • cheat in a way that ruins other players' matches, or use the service to attack, scan, overload or reverse-engineer our servers;
  • create accounts in bulk, evade a suspension or block, or send spam or friend-request floods;

You may delete your account at any time from the Online tab in the game. Deleting removes your data as described in the Privacy Policy.
