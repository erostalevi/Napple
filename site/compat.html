Compatibility lists

How titles behave on Napple

Reported, not guaranteed. These statuses are reported by the Napple developers and community from runs on their own Apple Silicon Macs (scripted runs, and play by a person where it says so). Other Macs, versions and updates can behave differently.

No titles are distributed. Napple ships no games, keys or firmware; every entry was built from a copy its tester owns. Napple is not affiliated with Nintendo.

The lists are generated from the repository (compat/matrix.json, the title notes and compat/online.json) by tools/support/gen_support.py. Platform coverage, which is about the toolchain rather than titles, is on the home page.
