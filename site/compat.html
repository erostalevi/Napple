Compatibility

How games behave on Napple

Reported, not guaranteed. Napple is in beta. These statuses come from runs on the developers' own Apple Silicon Macs (scripted runs, and play by a person where a note says so). Other Macs, versions and updates can behave differently.

No games are distributed. Napple ships no games, keys or firmware; every entry was built from a copy its tester owns. Napple is not affiliated with Nintendo.

The lists are generated from the repository by tools/support/gen_support.py. Platform coverage, which is about the toolchain rather than games, is on the home page.
