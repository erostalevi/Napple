# NSZ/XCZ ingest (zstd NCZ)

- New `napple_core::infrastructure::ncz`: `NczSource` presents an NCZ member as the original NCA (raw 0x4000 header, body zstd-decoded and re-encrypted with the per-section AES-CTR key/counter stored in the NCZ). `Nca::open` is unchanged.
- Streaming form (one zstd stream): forward-only decoder, backward seek restarts it; no full-RAM buffering. Block form (`NCZBLOCK`): random access, one block decoded per read (last cached).
- `.ncz` members are renamed `.nca` in `ingest::collect`, `napple-build` `Container::load` and `legacy.rs` dump path (one-line additions).
- compat `find_games` accepts `.nsz`/`.xcz`.
- Dependency: `zstd = "0.13"` in napple-core.
- Status: *tested* (synthetic NCZ round-trips, NSZ/XCZ end-to-end ingest). Not verified against a real retail .nsz.
