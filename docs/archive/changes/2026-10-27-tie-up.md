## SMO threads.c stall
Not fixed (**blocked: no reproduction**). In the last two end-to-end runs SMO's golden route went past the old stall point (14143 and 15451 frames, frames 8000/9000 captured, 0-1 gfx warnings); the stall after ~7,500 frames (a spinning guest thread holding a virtual core, no timer preemption) did not occur, so there was nothing to test a preemption fix against. The cores model (threads.c: a guest thread owns one of three virtual cores until it blocks; `preempt` is honoured only at svc entry) is the place; a fix needs a signal-based preemption point and a deterministic repro first.
