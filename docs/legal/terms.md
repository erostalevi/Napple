If your local law sets a higher age for consent to online services (for example 16 in some EU countries), you must be at least that age or have a parent or guardian's permission. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under the minimum age, we will delete it.

- One person, up to 8 profiles per account. Nicknames must be unique and must not impersonate other people, the Napple team or Nintendo.
