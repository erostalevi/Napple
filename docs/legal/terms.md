## What Napple is

Napple is a tool that converts game files you own into a native application for your own computer. It runs entirely on your machine.

## Your responsibilities

- You must own the console and the games, and dump your keys, firmware and titles yourself. Napple includes none of them and never downloads them.
- An application built by Napple contains game-derived content and is for your personal use only. Do not distribute it.
- Napple is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by Nintendo. Product names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to describe compatibility.
- Napple does not support piracy and does not circumvent anything on its own; it only processes files you supply.

## No online service

Napple has no online service. It does not contact Nintendo's servers and does not ask for Nintendo account credentials. Local play, split-screen and LAN play
between machines on your own network are the only multiplayer modes.
