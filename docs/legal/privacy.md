# Privacy notice

Napple collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry and no servers.

- Your keys, firmware, games, builds, saves and settings stay on your disk. Napple never uploads them.
- Napple makes no network connections on its own. LAN play uses your local network only when you start it.
- Downloading the engine or the app from GitHub is done by you in your browser, under GitHub's own privacy policy.
- The project website is a static page and sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
